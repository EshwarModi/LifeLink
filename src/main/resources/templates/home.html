How LifeLink Works

A streamlined 3-step system engineered for speed, privacy, and reliability during medical emergencies.

01

Register Profile

Register as a Blood Seeker or Blood Donor. Donors specify their blood group (A+, B+, O-, etc.), age, and availability status.

02

Post & Auto-Match

Seekers submit emergency requests with units needed, urgency, and hospital location. Our engine instantly notifies matching donors.

03

Match & Connect

Donors review matches and click "I Can Help". Phone numbers are securely disclosed only after match acceptance.

Privacy by Design Architecture

Your personal contact numbers are never published publicly. Phone details are strictly concealed until a matching donor explicitly accepts to help.
