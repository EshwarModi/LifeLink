How LifeLink Works

1

Register as Donor or Seeker

Create your account in under two minutes. Donors declare blood group and availability; patients and hospitals post urgent requests.

2

Post or Browse a Request

Hospitals specify required units and deadlines. Our algorithmic matching system immediately dispatches alerts to nearby eligible donors.

3

Match, Accept and Connect

An eligible donor clicks "I Can Help" to accept. Secure direct phone numbers are disclosed instantly for immediate hospital coordination.

Built with Clinical Integrity & Privacy by Design

LifeLink incorporates rigorous clinical protocols and strict privacy safeguards into every interaction.

Contact details stay hidden until a donor accepts

Phone numbers and direct contacts are protected. Private numbers are revealed only after an eligible donor formally accepts a match.

Only eligible donors are matched

Automated screening enforces the mandatory 90-day physiological whole-blood recovery interval and age requirements for donor safety.

Secure sign-in

BCrypt password hashing, Spring Security session control, audit trails, and end-to-end CSRF verification safeguard every operation.
